Hi everyone, we are team 402 Found: Elodie and me, Nera. Our project gives an AI agent pocket money with house rules, and gives a small online shop a way to get paid by agents. Everything you will see runs on Stellar testnet with test money.
The problem

AI agents are starting to buy things.

An agent can’t sign up or type a card number.

Five cents is too small for a card payment.

And if you just hand it your card, it’s like giving your whole wallet to someone you met last week.

Agents are starting to pay for things on their own: data, code fixes, small jobs. But an agent can’t create an account or type in a card number, and a five-cent job is too small for card fees. Giving the agent your real card is not safe either.
Our idea

Pocket money, with house rules.

“$2 a month, at most $0.25 a payment, only these shops.”

$2.00 a monthat most $0.25 a paymentonly listed shops

The owner writes the rules once, in plain words. The wallet turns them into checks in code. Over the limit, the agent stops and asks. Her own money stays in her own wallet.

How it works, in four steps →

What people want is what parents do with a teenager: a little pocket money and a few house rules. The owner writes the rules in one sentence. The wallet reads them once, shows exactly what it will enforce, and then checks every payment in code. There is no AI model in the payment step.

When it costs too much

It stops and asks.

A 50-cent report is over the 25-cent cap. The agent doesn’t pay. The receipt says Needs you: allow once, or say no.

Nothing is signed until the owner decides. A shop not on her list is simply blocked.

What if something costs more than the limit? Here the agent tried to buy a 50-cent report. The cap is 25 cents, so it stopped and asked, like a card asking “approve this purchase?”. Nothing is signed until the owner says yes. A shop that is not on the list is blocked outright.
Safe in layers

Even if one layer fails, the money stays safe.

Rules in code

Budget, cap per payment and allowed shops are checked in code before any signature. No AI model in the payment step.

An allowance on Stellar

The owner grants a monthly allowance on the USDC contract itself. It ends with the month. Past it, Stellar refuses the refill, even if our app had a bug.

Paid only on delivery

The shop settles only after the pull request is open. A failed or timed-out job is never charged, and retries return the same job.

See the allowance and its records on Pay a service.

Safety comes in three layers. One: the rules are checked in code, with no model involved. Two: the allowance also lives on Stellar itself, on the USDC contract, and it expires at the end of the month, so even a bug in our app cannot spend more. Three: my engine only settles after it has delivered the pull request.
